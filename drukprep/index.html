Questions

Is DrukPrep official or linked to the RCSC?

No. DrukPrep is an independent practice site and is not affiliated with the Royal Civil Service Commission. Always check exam dates, rules and the syllabus in the official announcements on rcsc.gov.bt.

Which sections are covered?

English, logical reasoning, data interpretation and general awareness. Dzongkha practice questions are being prepared and will be added to the Exam Pass at no extra cost.
