Questions

Is Soma really free?

Yes. You can create and download unlimited invoices for free. Free invoices include a small “Made with Soma” footer. Pro removes it and adds your logo, colors and saved clients.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored privately in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to our servers.

How do I download a PDF?

Click “Download PDF” and choose “Save as PDF” as the destination in the print dialog.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in one click from the billing portal — no questions asked. We also offer a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Is it legal to use these invoices?

Soma invoices contain all the standard fields. Tax rules vary by country, so check local requirements (e.g. VAT numbers) and add them in your details.
